Summary: SafmViewer does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Every measurement file you open is processed locally on your device and never leaves it.

1. Who we are

SafmViewer (“the App”) is developed and published by Semilab Zrt. (“we”, “us”), the data controller for the purposes of this policy.

  • Website: https://research-metrology.semilab.hu/
  • Contact e-mail: [Software@semilab.hu]
  • Postal address: [Prielle Kornélia u. 2. H-1117 Budapest, Hungary]

This policy explains how the App handles information. It applies only to the SafmViewer Android application, not to our websites or other products.

2. What personal data we collect

None. The App does not collect, request or receive any personal data. In particular, the App does not:

  • ask you to register, sign in or create an account;
  • collect your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact detail;
  • collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses or location data;
  • contain analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs;
  • track your behaviour in the App or across other apps and websites;
  • upload your files, or any part of their contents, to us or to anyone else.

Because no personal data is collected, none is stored on our servers, none is shared with third parties and none is sold.

3. How your files are handled

The purpose of the App is to open and visualise measurement files (Gwyddion-compatible Semilab AFM .safm files) that you explicitly select with the Android system file picker.

  • Files are read and rendered entirely on your device, in the App’s own memory.
  • The App has no server component and sends no file data anywhere.
  • The App does not copy your files, does not create hidden backups of them and does not modify them.
  • The App can only access the specific file you pick; it does not scan or index your storage.

If your measurement files happen to contain personal or confidential information, that information stays on your device and remains solely under your control.

4. Data stored on your device

The App saves a small amount of non-personal setting data locally, using the standard Android app-preferences storage:

Stored itemPurposeLeaves the device?
Selected colour map (e.g. for heat-map rendering) Remembers your display preference between sessions No

This data contains no personal information and is removed when you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

5. Android permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy the App requests it
INTERNET Only to hand a link over to your browser or to the Google Play app when you tap one (for example the Gwyddion, Semilab, open-source-library or “Rate app” links). The App itself makes no network requests and sends no data over the internet.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE To check whether a network connection is available before opening such a link.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below only) To read the measurement file you selected yourself in the file picker. On Android 13 and above this permission is not requested at all.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, location, photos or call data.

6. Third parties

The App integrates no third-party data-collecting services. Two situations nevertheless involve parties other than us:

  • External links. When you tap a link in the App (for example gwyddion.net, GitHub, our website, or the Play Store listing), your device opens it in your browser or in the Google Play app. From that moment on, the destination site or app may receive technical data such as your IP address and browser user agent, and its own privacy policy applies. We receive nothing from these visits.
  • Distribution via Google Play. The App is distributed by Google Play. Google processes installation, purchase-free download and (if you have opted in to it in your Android settings) automatic crash and performance reporting under its own Google Privacy Policy. Any such reports go to Google, in aggregated and anonymised form, and are outside the App’s own code. We do not combine them with any other data.

The App also contains the open-source libraries SkiaSharp and ScottPlot, used purely for local rendering. They perform no network communication and collect no data.

7. Children

The App is a professional measurement-data viewer and is not directed at children. Since it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

8. Security

The strongest protection the App offers is architectural: your data never leaves your device, so there is no transmission to intercept and no server-side store to breach. Your files remain protected by your device’s own operating-system security and by Android app sandboxing.

9. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable laws you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase. You can remove all locally stored settings at any time via Android Settings → Apps → SafmViewer → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App.

If you believe your data has nevertheless been processed unlawfully, you may contact us at the address in section 1 and you may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App’s functionality changes in a way that affects data handling, we will update this policy and revise the “Last updated” date at the top of the page. Material changes will also be reflected in the App’s Google Play listing. Please review this page occasionally.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in the App: Software@semilab.hu — Semilab Zrt.

Az Alkalmazás célja az Ön által – az Android rendszer fájlválasztójával – kifejezetten kiválasztott mérési fájlok (Gwyddion-kompatibilis Semilab AFM .safm fájlok) megnyitása és megjelenítése.

  • A fájlok beolvasása és megjelenítése teljes egészében a készüléken, az Alkalmazás memóriájában történik.
  • Az Alkalmazásnak nincs kiszolgálói oldala, és nem küld fájladatot sehová.
  • Az Alkalmazás nem másolja a fájljait, nem készít róluk rejtett biztonsági mentést, és nem módosítja őket.
  • Az Alkalmazás kizárólag a kiválasztott fájlhoz kap hozzáférést; nem vizsgálja át és nem indexeli a tárhelyet.

Ha a mérési fájljai személyes vagy bizalmas információt tartalmaznak, az az információ a készülékén marad, és továbbra is kizárólag az Ön ellenőrzése alatt áll.
